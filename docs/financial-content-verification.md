## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

During the Phase 1 audit and subsequent Phase 5 content reconciliations, legacy marketing materials and external web references associated with C.P. Arora were reviewed to evaluate statements concerning annual turnover, revenue growth, and balance-sheet net worth.

The audit identified significant discrepancies, unverified claims, and outdated data points across legacy drafts:
1. **Unverified Consolidated Claim:** An unsubstantiated figure of *"₹700 Crore Annual Turnover"* appeared in promotional blurbs without audit certification, chartered accountant certification, or MCA-filed balance sheets.
2. **Outdated Historical Snapshots:** Legacy drafts referenced financial figures from FY 2021–22 or earlier without continuity or audited disclosures for subsequent fiscal years (FY 2022–23, FY 2023–24, FY 2024–25).
3. **Absence of Audited Statements in Local Archive:** The preserved local workspace assets (`assets/`, `site_assets/`, `preserved_assets/`) contain statutory contractor enlistments and photographic equipment logs, but do **not** contain complete audited balance sheets, profit & loss accounts, or ROC annual return filings (Form MGT-7 / AOC-4).

In compliance with the project's zero-fabrication and statutory integrity principles, **no unverified financial metrics or legacy turnover estimates are published on `/financials`**. Instead, the page presents documented departmental tendering capacities, banking framework disclosures, and an official protocol for institutional verification.

## 3. Editorial & Governance Policy for `/financials`

1. **No Speculative Financial Metrics:**
   - The rebuild will **not** display self-reported turnover, revenue charts, profit after tax (PAT), or EBIDTA margins until certified Form 3CA/3CB/3CD tax audits or MCA annual returns are officially integrated.
2. **Distinction Between Capacity and Revenue:**
   - Tendering limits granted by government bodies (e.g. Goa PWD "No Limit", MCD "₹20 Crore", NBCC "₹25–₹50 Cr") are statutory qualification thresholds, not revenue figures. The rebuild labels them explicitly as **"Approved Departmental Tendering Ceilings"**.
3. **Official Bid Document Verification Protocol:**
   - For public tenders, institutional bids, and joint ventures, authenticated copies of:
     - CA-certified Turnover Certificates with UDIN (Unique Document Identification Number),
     - Bank Solvency Certificates,
     - Audited Balance Sheets and P&L Accounts with auditor notes,
     - GST and EPF/ESIC clearance certificates,
   are furnished directly to client authorities via the corporate Contracts & Estimation Desk upon formal Notice Inviting Tender (NIT) request.
4. **Transparency Notice on the Financials Page:**
   - An explicit, professional notice is included explaining that public disclosure of unaudited or outdated financial statements is restricted to preserve statutory compliance and competitive confidentiality.

4. Net Worth Certificate certified by the statutory auditor.

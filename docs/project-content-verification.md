# CPA Arora — Project Content Verification & Reconciliation Audit

This document details the source verification, data reconciliation, and audit policy governing the infrastructure project portfolio displayed across `/projects`, `/ongoing-projects`, `/executed-projects`, and `/major-projects`.

---

## 1. Executive Reconciliation Policy

In adherence to strict engineering integrity guidelines:
1. **Zero Fabrication:** No contract valuation, financial turnover, project count, client endorsement, or completion date is invented.
2. **Technical Scope Separation:** Where photographic evidence and departmental records confirm technical civil execution (e.g., asphalt paving, milling, friction courses, sensor paver deployment), technical scopes are preserved while unverified financial figures are omitted.
3. **Status Partitioning:**
   - **Ongoing (`/ongoing-projects`):** Only works with active execution records and verified highway/civil scopes.
   - **Executed (`/executed-projects`):** Projects confirmed as completed deliverables handed over to municipal or public works authorities.
   - **Landmark (`/major-projects`):** Significant national corridor packages and aviation airside works confirmed by photographic registries and departmental association.

## 3. Conflicting Information & Reconciled Audit Decisions

The Phase 1 repository audit flagged four specific project packages with historical discrepancies across legacy sheets and marketing copies:

### A. NH-16 Odisha (Puintola–Tangi Section)
- **Discrepancy:** Legacy files reported contradictory contract values (varying between ₹140 Cr and unverified figures) and differing completion years.
- **Resolution in Rebuild:** The project is cataloged based solely on its confirmed technical scope: 4-laning flexible pavement construction, DBM/BC paving to MoRTH Section 500, and cross-drainage infrastructure. All unverified financial figures and disputed milestone dates have been omitted from the public presentation.

### B. Delhi–Gurugram Expressway (NH-8)
- **Discrepancy:** Multiple conflicting maintenance terms, chainages, and turnover figures were cited across legacy draft sheets.
- **Resolution in Rebuild:** Treated as a periodic pavement rehabilitation and corridor maintenance contract. Documented technical methodology (nocturnal cold milling, polymer micro-surfacing, and live-traffic diversion) is emphasized while speculative contract revenue figures are excluded.

### C. Indira Gandhi International Airport (IGI Airport) Packages
- **Discrepancy:** Work at Delhi Airport was executed under multiple separate task orders and work-package authorizations from GMR/DIAL, leading to inconsistent cumulative valuations.
- **Resolution in Rebuild:** Partitioned cleanly into confirmed airside disciplines (Runway Friction Course Overlays and Taxiway/Apron Rehabilitation) with verified night-closure operational protocols. Unverified consolidated turnover claims are omitted.

### D. NH-544 Kerala (Thrissur–Edappally Corridor)
- **Discrepancy:** Chainage markings and sub-contract financial amounts differed across legacy spreadsheets.
- **Resolution in Rebuild:** Documented for its verified engineering characteristics: monsoon-resilient bituminous wearing courses and high-rainfall sub-surface drainage in rolling terrain. Unreconciled financial values remain excluded.
